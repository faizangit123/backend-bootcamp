My login flow:
1.Final flow (simple)
2.User sends username/email + password.
3.Server finds the user.
4.Server hashes the entered password.
5.Server compares the hash with the stored hash.
6.If they match, create a JWT.
7.Store the JWT in a cookie.
8.Return only safe user information (username, email, bio, profilePic, etc.)—never the password, even if it's hashed.
